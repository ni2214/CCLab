At Your Step

This prototype is a small interactive scene set on the Mongolian steppe, where the player makes five choices for their tribe and watches the land respond. A trader wants the last pines. The herd needs somewhere to graze. A river could be diverted. Each decision either harms the steppe or spares it, and the scene slowly reshapes itself in answer: the sky warms or sours, green fades into dry yellow, trees thin out across the ridge, and the air fills with dust or drifting pollen. By the end, the steppe is either enduring, wounded, or barren. The prototype exists to test the heart of the bigger experience: whether a single landscape can feel alive and responsive enough to carry the weight of the player's choices, before any of the surrounding world systems are built.
