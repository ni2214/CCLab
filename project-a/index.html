LumenWorm

Scientific Name:

Oscillata lumina subterranea

Discovered by / at:

Discovered during Chinese New Year by a random guy walking barefeet on soil

Family:

Oscillatarranea

Habitat:

Subterranean soil environments influenced by fluctuating light and heat sources.

Appearance:

A soft, segmented worm-like organism composed of circular forms connected by flexible lines. It has a slightly enlarged head with small eyes and a subtle mouth. Its body pulses rhythmically and moves fluidly through an earthy, textured environment.

Key Characteristics:

  • Moves using sine-based oscillation
  • Body segments follow head through vector math
  • Attracted to food stimuli
  • Repelled by light/heat sources
  • Exhibits pulsing and curving motion
  • Color varies within earthy HSB tones

What we know so far about LumenWorm? LumenWorm responds to environmental forces rather than following a fixed path. Its movement emerges from oscillation and steering behaviors. It seeks food while avoiding light, creating tension between attraction and repulsion that shapes its survival behavior.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature: Clicking places food in the environment, which attracts the worm. The cursor emits a pulsing light that repels it. Users must balance feeding the creature while keeping distance, indirectly guiding its motion through environmental influence rather than direct control.
